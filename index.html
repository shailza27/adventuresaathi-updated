Who we are

Adventure Saathi started with a simple complaint: most trips felt like they were run for a spreadsheet, not for the people on the trail.

We're a small team of guides, drivers, cooks and route-planners who grew up in the mountains we now take you into. Some of us started as porters, some as trekkers who never really came back down. What we share is a refusal to hand you a laminated itinerary and call it a trip.

Every route we run — trek or road journey — is scouted by someone on our team first. We keep groups small, build in slack for weather and altitude, and stay close by phone and WhatsApp from the day you book to the day you're back home telling everyone about it.
